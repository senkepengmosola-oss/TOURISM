About Lesotho

Discover the Kingdom in the Sky

Welcome to Lesotho 🇱🇸

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom known for its breathtaking landscapes, rich culture and unique traditions.

Known as the Kingdom in the Sky, Lesotho offers visitors opportunities to experience nature, adventure and Basotho culture.

From spectacular mountains and waterfalls to historical sites and traditional villages, Lesotho offers something special for every traveller.

What Makes Lesotho Special?

🏔️ Mountains

Lesotho is famous for its spectacular mountain landscapes and high-altitude scenery.

👑 Basotho Culture

Visitors can experience Basotho traditions, clothing, food, music and ways of life.

🐎 Adventure

Lesotho offers exciting outdoor activities including hiking, horse riding and other mountain adventures.
