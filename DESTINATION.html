Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls is one of the spectacular natural attractions associated with Lesotho's mountainous landscape.

Visitors can enjoy the surrounding scenery and outdoor experiences.

Thaba-Bosiu

Thaba-Bosiu is an important historical and cultural destination in Lesotho.

Visitors can learn more about the history and heritage associated with the site.

Katse Dam

Katse Dam is surrounded by spectacular mountain scenery and is an important destination for visitors exploring Lesotho's highlands.

Bokong Nature Reserve

Bokong Nature Reserve offers visitors beautiful highland scenery and an opportunity to experience Lesotho's natural environment.

Sehlabathebe National Park

Sehlabathebe National Park is known for its dramatic natural landscapes and beautiful highland environment.

Afriski Mountain Resort

Afriski is a mountain destination where visitors can enjoy winter activities and the spectacular highland environment.
